Getting Started

Logging in, onboarding, and navigating the system

First-Time Login (Onboarding)

When your account is created, you'll receive an email with a setup link

1

Click the onboarding link in your email from the administrator

2

You'll see the "Set Up Your Password" page — enter a new password (at least 8 characters)

3

Confirm your password by typing it again, then click "Complete Setup"

4

You'll see a success message — you'll be redirected to the Login page

💡

Tip: Use a mix of letters, numbers, and special characters for a strong password.

Regular Login & Password Reset

How to sign in every day and recover a forgotten password

1

Open your browser and go to the PMS website address

2

Enter your email address and password on the Sign In page

3

Click "Sign in" — you'll land on your Dashboard

⚠️

Session Timeout: Your session expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. You'll see a notification and be redirected to the login page.

🔑 Forgot Password?

  • Click "Forgot your password?" on the login page
  • Enter your email address and submit
  • You'll receive a password reset link via email
  • Click the link and set a new password

Goals

Setting, tracking, and scoring performance goals

Goal Types

There are three types of goals, each with different visibility and creation rules

For employees: New individual goals start as "Pending Approval" and need your supervisor's sign-off before they become active.

Creating an Individual Goal

How to set your own performance goal

1

Go to Goals → click "+ Create Goal"

2

Enter a Goal Title (clear and specific, e.g., "Increase customer satisfaction by 15%")

3

Add a Description (optional) — use the rich text editor for detail

4

Set Difficulty Level (1=Very Easy to 5=Very Hard) — this weights the goal in your scorecard

5

Add KPIs — click "Add KPI" for each measurable target (description + target value + unit)

Creating a Goal (continued)

Setting the time period and optional details

6

Select Time Period: Yearly or Quarterly (if Quarterly, pick the quarter and year)

7

Optionally set Start Date and End Date

8

Optionally link to a parent organizational goal (departmental goals that cascade down)

9

Optionally add Tags to categorize the goal

10

Click "Create Goal" — it will appear as Pending Approval until your supervisor approves it

⚠️

Important: New individual goals need supervisor approval. Contact your supervisor if they haven't reviewed it.

Every goal goes through these statuses

Updating Goal Progress & Completing

How to track your progress throughout the period

1

Go to Goals → find your active goal → click the ⋮ menu

2

Select "Update Progress" — enter a percentage (0–100) and write a progress report

3

At 100%, you'll also fill in KPI Actuals (the actual values you achieved for each KPI) and an optional comment

4

Click "Mark as Complete" — the goal status changes to Completed

Other Goal Actions:

  • Request Change — Ask your supervisor to modify an active goal
  • Accept / Decline — Respond when a supervisor assigns you a goal
  • Discard Goal — Remove a goal you no longer need

Initiatives

Assigning, tracking, and completing work assignments

What Are Initiatives?

Initiatives are specific work assignments that contribute toward your goals

📋 Individual Initiative

  • Assigned to one person
  • You can assign it to yourself or a colleague
  • Has a title, description, priority, and due date
  • Can optionally be linked to a goal
  • Can have sub-tasks for checkable milestones

👥 Group Initiative

  • Assigned to 2 or more people
  • Requires a Team Head who submits on behalf of the group
  • Same fields as individual, plus team member selection
  • Everyone can see the initiative in their list
  • Team Head is responsible for final submission
💡

Think of it this way: A Goal defines what you want to achieve. An Initiative defines how you'll achieve it.

Creating an Initiative

Step-by-step guide to creating a new work assignment

1

Go to Initiatives → click "+ Create Initiative"

2

Enter a Title and optional Description

3

Select Type: Individual or Group

4

Set Priority: Low, Medium, High, or Urgent

5

Choose a Due Date (must be in the future)

6

Optionally link to a Goal and add Sub-tasks

7

Optionally attach documents (up to 5 files, 10MB each)

8

Click "Create"

Initiative Status Lifecycle

Every initiative goes through these stages

StatusMeaningWho Acts Next
Pending ApprovalCreated by employee, awaiting supervisorSupervisor: approve or reject
AssignedAssigned to someone, waiting to be acceptedAssignee: accept the initiative
PendingAccepted, ready to begin workAssignee: click "Start Initiative"
OngoingWork is in progressAssignee: submit when done
Under ReviewSubmitted for supervisor reviewSupervisor: grade and provide feedback
ApprovedSupervisor approved with a gradeInitiative is complete
RejectedSupervisor rejected the submissionAssignee: address feedback and resubmit
OverduePast the due date without completionAssignee: submit ASAP

Initiative Workflow: Two Perspectives

Different actions depending on your role

👤 As the Assignee (Employee)

  • Receive the initiative assignment
  • Accept it if status is "Assigned"
  • Start working on it
  • Track sub-tasks by checking them off
  • Submit with a completion report + files
  • If rejected, read the redo instructions and resubmit

👥 As the Supervisor (Reviewer)

  • Create & assign initiatives to supervisees
  • Approve/reject initiatives pending approval
  • When status is "Under Review", click "Review Initiative"
  • Select a Grade (1–10):
    1–3 = Poor | 4–6 = Fair | 7–8 = Good | 9–10 = Excellent
  • Enter feedback (optional for approval, required for rejection)
  • Click "Accept & Grade" or "Reject"

Scorecards

How goals are scored and performance is evaluated

How Goal Scoring Works

Supervisors rate completed goals on a 0–5 scale

Supervisor Score (0–5)

0Not achieved at all
1Very poor — far below expectations
2Below expectations
3Meets expectations
4Exceeds expectations
5Outstanding performance

KPI Auto-Scoring

If a goal has KPIs defined, the system calculates a KPI score automatically:

KPI Score = min(Actual ÷ Target, 1) × 5

Example: Target = 30, Actual = 27
Score = min(27/30, 1) × 5 = 4.5 / 5

How to Score a Goal (Supervisor)

When an employee marks a goal as "Completed", you score it

1

Go to Goals → Team tab and find goals with Completed status

2

Click the ⋮ menu → "Score Goal"

3

Review the employee's KPI actuals and their comment

4

Enter your Score (0–5) and an optional Comment

5

Optionally set an Achievement Override: "Mark as Achieved", "Not Achieved", or "Auto" (let the system decide)

6

Click "Submit Score"

Overall Performance Score Formula

How the system calculates your final performance rating

Review Score per Trait

Self × 20% + Peer × 30% + Supervisor × 50%

Each performance trait gets a weighted average of ratings from different reviewers.

Overall Performance

Task Performance × 60% + Review Performance × 40%

Combines initiative/task grades with review scores.

Performance Reviews

Formal assessments conducted during review cycles

Completing a Self-Review

How to evaluate your own performance during a review cycle

1

Go to Reviews in the sidebar

2

Select a Review Cycle from the dropdown (e.g., "Q1 2025 Performance Review")

3

Under the Self tab, find your self-review assignment

4

Click "Start Review" — you'll see questions organized by trait

5

For each question, select a rating (1–10) and add comments if available

6

Your progress saves automatically — you can leave and come back

7

When all questions are answered, the review is marked Completed

Review Types

Two types of reviews happen during each cycle

📝 Self-Review

  • You evaluate your own performance
  • Answer questions about your work, skills, and achievements
  • Rating scale: 1–10 per question
  • Organized by traits (e.g., Communication, Leadership)
  • Self scores carry 20% weight

👥 Supervisor Review

  • Your supervisor evaluates your performance
  • Same traits and questions, but from the manager's perspective
  • Rating scale: 1–10 per question
  • Supervisor scores carry 50% weight
  • Found under the "Supervisee" tab
💡

Review Cycles are created by administrators and can be Quarterly, Annual, Probationary, or Project-based. You'll receive a notification when a review cycle is launched.

Organization Hierarchy & User Management

How the organizational structure works and user lifecycle

🏢 5-Level Org Hierarchy

🌍 Global (Company-wide)
  └── 🏛️ Directorate (e.g., Operations)
     └── 🏢 Department (e.g., HR)
        └── 📁 Division
           └── 👥 Unit (Teams)

Users at higher levels can see data from all levels below them.

👤 User Account Statuses

  • Pending Activation — Created but hasn't set password
  • Active — Fully set up and can use the system
  • Suspended — Temporarily disabled
  • On Leave — User is on leave
  • Archived — Permanently deactivated

🔑 Scope Options

  • None — Access limited to own department and below
  • Global — Access to all data across the system
  • Cross-Directorate — Access across multiple directorates

FAQ & Glossary

Common questions and key terms

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions from staff

Why is my goal showing "Pending Approval"?

All new individual goals need supervisor approval before becoming active. Contact your supervisor.

Can I edit a goal after it's approved?

Use "Request Change" on an active goal. Your supervisor will review the request.

What are KPIs and do I have to add them?

KPIs are measurable targets. They're optional but recommended — e.g., "Achieve 30% increase" with target 30 and unit %.

What's the difference between a Goal and an Initiative?

A Goal defines WHAT you want to achieve. An Initiative defines HOW you'll achieve it.

Can I assign an initiative to a group?

Yes! Select "Group" as the type, choose at least 2 members, and designate a Team Head.

What happens when I submit an initiative?

Your supervisor receives a notification and will grade it (1–10) with feedback. Approved = complete. Rejected = redo with instructions.

When will I receive a review assignment?

When your administrator launches a review cycle. You'll get a notification.

Can I save my review and come back later?

Yes! Progress is saved automatically. You can leave and return at any time.

Glossary of Key Terms

Quick reference for PMS terminology

PMS

Performance Management System — the application you're using

Goal

A performance target set for a specific period (yearly, quarterly, or departmental)

KPI

Key Performance Indicator — a measurable target that tracks progress (e.g., "30% increase" with target 30, unit %)

Initiative

A specific work assignment or task that may be linked to a goal

Scorecard

A comprehensive evaluation combining goal scores, initiative grades, and review ratings

Review Cycle

A defined period during which performance reviews are conducted (Quarterly, Annual, etc.)

Self-Review

An assessment where you evaluate your own performance

Supervisor Review

An assessment where your manager evaluates your performance

Trait

A performance dimension (e.g., Communication, Leadership) evaluated during reviews

Difficulty Level

A rating (1–5) that weights a goal's importance in the overall scorecard
